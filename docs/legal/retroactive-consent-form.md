# Retroactive Consent — SRC2 Contributors and Participants

**Status: DRAFT FOR REVIEW. Not legal advice.**

For people who already contributed to or participated in SRC2 (27 April – 27 July 2026)
under the original Volunteering Agreement, which contains no attribution or image
release. Sent after the fact, it must therefore be genuinely easy to decline.

## Why this is needed

64 media assets — 26 videos and 38 photographs — were captured during SRC2. Fifty
participants attended. None of them agreed to be published, because no agreement covering
publication existed.

Without this, none of that material can appear on the website, and no participant can be
named. With it, the evidence gallery and the SRC1-alumni pipeline story both become
publishable.

## How to run it

- **Deliver it as a Google Form**, matching the existing SRC2 workflow, one response per person.
- **Default every option to "no".** Consent obtained by pre-ticked boxes is not consent, and a form that reads as a formality invites people to click through without deciding.
- **Silence means no.** Anyone who does not respond is not published. Do not chase.
- **Send it individually**, not to a group channel. A public request creates social pressure to agree, which contaminates the consent.
- **Keep the responses**, but store them outside the website repository — they are personal data, and everything in §7 of the knowledge-base analysis applies.
- **Photographs of identifiable minors**, if any participants were under 18, need guardian consent. Check the applicant ages before sending; the applicant data records age.

## What to do with non-responses

Publish nothing for that person. In practice this means:

- Their name renders as `withheld` on the site
- Group photographs in which they are identifiable cannot be published
- Their contribution is still recorded internally, and still counts toward the contribution threshold for a person page, should they later consent

You contributed to the Applied Scientific Research Camp, Edition II (Aleppo, April–July
2026). We are building the Boundless website, where the work of this camp will be

We did not ask your permission at the time. We are asking now, and **you can say no to
any part of this with no consequence at all.** Saying no does not affect your

If you do not reply, we will treat that as "no" and publish nothing about you.
