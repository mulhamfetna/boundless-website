**Boundless Academic Services** helps students and researchers unleash their true
academic potential.

## Our vision

To become the first applied research institution in Syria: one that brings
universities, civil society and industry together around research that serves
society.

## What we do

- **Scientific Research** — a full training curriculum delivered by our own trainer
  team, followed by research fellowships across applied, medical, humanities and
  data fields.
- **Scholarship Mentorship** — for high-school students, undergraduates, students
  already in university, and graduates.
- **Academic & Global Curricula** — SAT and other international curricula and exam
  preparation.

Our programmes run from one-to-one mentorship through group mentorship, general
sessions and workshops. Some are paid; others are delivered entirely by volunteers,
free of charge.

## How we work

- **Project by project.** Each programme has its own team, partners and plan. We
  work with governmental, non-governmental, non-profit and private-sector bodies.
- **From idea to publication.** Our research camps don't stop at training: they
  continue into a supervised fellowship that aims at a paper in a peer-reviewed
  journal.
- **Three destinations for research.** We connect research to the academic path,
  to the labour market, and to the economic feasibility of projects.
- **Graduates who lead.** Graduates of our first camp became the organising team
  of the second.

## Our network

Through the German-Syrian Research Society (DSFG), the Syrian Professional Network
and other Syrian research and professional networks, we are connected with more
than 160 Syrian PhD researchers around the world.
