**Boundless Academic Services** helps students and researchers unleash their true
academic potential.

A youth academic community that started at the University of Aleppo and is
expanding across Syria.

## Our mission and goal

Our core mission is **interdisciplinary research**. Our long-term goal is to bring
academic institutions, civil society organisations and industry to one table, and
to become the first applied research institution in Syria.

## What we do

- **Scientific Research** — a full training curriculum delivered by our own trainer
  team, followed by research fellowships across applied, medical, humanities and
  data fields.
- **Scholarship Mentorship** — for high-school students, undergraduates, students
  already in university, and graduates.
- **Academic & Global Curricula** — SAT and other international curricula and exam
  preparation.

Our programmes run from one-to-one mentorship through group mentorship, general
sessions and workshops. Some are paid; others are delivered entirely by volunteers,
free of charge.

## How we work

- **A volunteer team.** Our team is made up entirely of volunteers.
- **Project by project.** Each project has its own partners, trainers and
  programme. Across our three projects we have worked with university professors,
  experts and people from industry.
- **Connecting sectors.** Over the past two years we have, to a degree, connected
  the NGO sector with the academic sector. Both research camps were incubated by
  Sanad Youth and supported and funded by UNFPA Syria.
- **From idea to publication.** Our research camps don't stop at training: they
  continue into a supervised fellowship that aims at a paper in a peer-reviewed
  journal.
- **Three destinations for research.** We connect research to the academic path,
  to the labour market, and to the economic feasibility of projects.
- **Graduates who lead.** Graduates of our first camp became the organising team
  of the second.

## Our network

Through our founder's academic and professional network, we are connected with more
than 160 Syrian PhD researchers in Syria and around the world who support our
projects.
