We asked participants of the first Scientific Research Camp about their experience, and about the ideas they believed before they started. Here are the main ones, with what they discovered for themselves.

| Myth | Reality |
|---|---|
| Publishing in an international journal is impossible for a beginner. | Publishing is possible once you understand the right steps and standards. |
| Without resources and support, you can't start. | A lack of resources can push you towards alternative solutions and smarter ideas. |
| Any study takes two years or more. | How long a study takes depends on its type, aim and method. |
| Research has to be a huge invention. | Science builds up step by step; sometimes a simple question creates real value. |
| A researcher has to be a genius. | A researcher is an ordinary person who learns the skill and improves it through practice. |
| Research is all about creativity. | Research is method, steps and organised, repeated work. |
| Research will take over your life. | Research can become part of your routine. |
| I need to know everything before I start. | It's enough to start from one specific part of your field. |

What is certain is that science moves forward in small steps, taken by researchers who started with a single question.
