Today we celebrate with you the end of training for the second cohort of the Scientific Research Camp.

Forty training hours carried many adventures across the worlds of applied scientific research, data analysis, and building researchers' professional digital presence.

## Thanks to the trainers

We thank the trainers, who played a key role in this project's success through rich scientific content, training hours, and support that continues into the research fellowship and the incubation of research projects. Your efforts were a key factor in our success.

- Foundations of applied scientific research: Dr. Abdulrazzaq Hammal
- Statistics and data analysis for researchers: Ms. Mazyat Kourani
- Building researchers' professional digital presence: Eng. Mulham Fetna

## Thanks to our partners

Our full thanks to our partners for their funding, incubation, logistical and advisory support. They gave generously and showed cooperation and flexibility that deserve praise.

- Logistical support: Sanad Youth, Domains Center
- Financial support: UNFPA Syria

## The volunteer team

We also thank the initiative's volunteer team, our greatest pride: most of the second cohort's team are graduates of the camp's first cohort. This is an achievement we are proud of: the initiative sustains itself and keeps renewing, by activating and empowering a research community that shares the same values and interests and a sense of social responsibility.

## To the second cohort's graduates

We are proud of everyone who graduated from this camp. The training was not easy: forty hours of dense academic content. We take our hats off to you.

Yesterday's trainees are tomorrow's partners in success. We welcome you as active researchers in our research community, as volunteers in our coming projects, and as community partners.

Our role in empowering young people academically does not cancel our need to learn from the experience of the community we have built. This is our strength: mutual empowerment.

The goal was never training that simply ends, but building an interdisciplinary research community that is active and productive.

## After the training

Today we close the training of the second cohort and open the doors of the research fellowship (incubation) phase.

We are proud of this achievement. While several research communities exist in specific fields, we were the first and largest community empowering interdisciplinary scientific research. Our research output has begun, and it will continue.

Watch for a series of online workshops and lectures, to be announced soon.
