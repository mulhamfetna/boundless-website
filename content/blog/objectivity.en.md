## What is objectivity?

Seeing a phenomenon as it is, without personal bias, interests or prejudgement.

## Why is it hard?

Because every researcher is shaped by:

- their own assumptions,
- their social environment,
- the way they think.

## How is it achieved?

- Through measurement and numbers.
- By using consistent tools and standards.
- By relying on evidence, not opinion.

## Objectivity is not "removing the human"

Science doesn't cancel out the researcher; it tries to keep their influence in check through the scientific method. An objective researcher doesn't cling to a view once it's shown to be wrong. They doubt, criticise, review and test everything, including their own ideas.

So objectivity is not a perfect state. It's an ongoing practice of reducing bias as much as possible. Keep that in mind while you write.
