When you read a scientific study, your attention usually goes to the results. But every study holds a treasure that most readers miss.

## Look for these sentences

- "This study did not address..."
- "Future research is recommended on..."
- "A limitation of the present study is..."
- "Further research is needed into..."

## Why they matter

These are not routine phrases. They point straight to:

- research gaps nobody has covered yet,
- questions that are still unanswered,
- ideas that could become new research.

A good researcher doesn't only read studies; they look for what is missing from them. A whole research idea can be hiding in the "Recommendations" section.

**Try it now:** open the last study you read in your field and reread its "Limitations" and "Recommendations" sections with fresh eyes.
