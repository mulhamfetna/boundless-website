If you think scientific research is boring, it's probably because you learned it as a university assignment rather than as a way to understand the world.

## What research is not

Research is not just "collecting information", and it isn't copying from references.

## So what is it?

It is a systematic, organised process of collecting data and analysing it objectively, in order to advance knowledge or solve real problems.

In other words, a researcher doesn't stop at observing. They:

- ask,
- analyse,
- test,
- and draw conclusions.

## Every study starts with a question

That question becomes a study, then an analysis, then results, then new knowledge. That's why research is more than a degree requirement: it is a skill that gives you a new way of thinking.
