> "The university doesn't even have whiteboard markers... and you still want to run a research camp?!"

That sentence is where our journey started. It summed up the reality of limited resources, and it became the fuel for our determination. Today we are proud to announce the close of the "Scientific Research Camp - First Cohort" in Aleppo, showing that creative minds are not limited by resources.

Led by **Eng. Mulham Fetna**, founder and CEO of Boundless, we honoured the partners of this success: our dedicated team, our expert lecturers, and our partner organisations in Aleppo who believed in our vision.

## What did we achieve in 40 training hours?

Over 11 intensive workshops, we targeted 75 graduates and postgraduate students of the University of Aleppo. The result: original research that is now close to publication in leading peer-reviewed journals.

## The modules that made the difference

1. **Research Design**: generating original research ideas and filling scientific gaps.
2. **Scientific Publishing**: how to choose international journals, and publication requirements.
3. **Applied Research**: research methods in low-resource settings.
4. **Statistical Analysis**: from descriptive statistics to advanced data analysis.
5. **Economic Integration**: feasibility studies and linking research to the job market.
6. **Digital Presence**: building the researcher's digital identity (LinkedIn and Favikon).
7. **Career Tools**: the differences between academic and professional CVs, and elevator pitch skills.
8. **AI in Research**: prompt engineering and specialised AI tools.

## What comes next?

Do we stop here? Never. Boundless lives up to its name: there are no limits to our ambition.

Very soon we will reveal our plan for 2026, which will include:

- details of the next stages of the Scientific Research Camp
- how to join the new camps
- the launch of membership in our professional research community, so you can be part of a network of researchers across borders

Stay tuned: what comes next is bigger.
