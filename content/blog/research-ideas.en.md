A research idea doesn't just land on you. It grows out of a question, an observation or plain curiosity. So where do research ideas come from?

## 1. Reading studies critically

Ask: what hasn't been researched yet? Where did earlier studies stop?

## 2. Real, everyday problems

Which things happening around you need to be understood better?

## 3. Technological change

How is technology changing people and society, and what new questions does that raise?

## 4. Scientific conversation

What question came up in a discussion with a colleague or professor that nobody could answer?

---

**Our question to you:** what topic or phenomenon is making you curious right now, and do you think it deserves a scientific study?
