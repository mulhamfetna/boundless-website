Before you submit your paper, make sure you haven't made any of these mistakes.

1. **Falsifying data or images.** Changing results or reporting data that isn't real destroys your credibility.
2. **Duplicate publication.** Sending the same paper to more than one journal at once, or publishing it twice, is academic misconduct.
3. **Ignoring conflicts of interest.** Anything that could influence the research must be clearly declared.
4. **Leaving out contributors.** Everyone who actually contributed to the work must be named and credited.
5. **Obvious methodological errors.** A weak design or missing information casts doubt on the quality of the study.
6. **Inaccurate quotations and references.** Copying without citing, or citing sources that have nothing to do with the work.
7. **Weak language and literal translation.** Even a strong idea can be rejected because the writing is unclear.
8. **Ignoring the journal's instructions.** Not following the required format or files can lead to a desk rejection.

Save this list and come back to it before every submission.
