Almost everyone who has written a paper has been through these stages. If you're in one of them right now, you're not alone.

1. **Excitement.** At first, your research idea feels special and strong.
2. **Lost.** After reading dozens of studies you ask yourself, "What am I even writing?"
3. **Self-doubt.** Is my research strong enough? Does my idea matter? Can I finish this?
4. **Exhaustion.** Endless references and endless revisions.
5. **Frustration.** The results come out the opposite of what you expected.
6. **Achievement.** You write the last line and watch the paper come together.

These stages are a normal part of research, not a sign that you aren't cut out for it. That's why our camps work in teams, with academic supervisors who stay with you at every stage.
