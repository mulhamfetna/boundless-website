After months of continuous work, and through the efforts of 26 researchers from the Universities of Aleppo and Hama, we completed and published our paper in Hybrid Advances, a Q2 journal, titled

> Design and evaluation of triple-doped nano-hydroxyapatite (Mg/Si/Zn-HAP) as multi-step intelligent scaffold for bone healing

This paper is the result of a collaboration without precedent in Syria in its number of members and range of disciplines. All of them are enthusiastic young people, most of them not yet past graduation age.

## First: the team and how we worked together

The team was 26 researchers from the Universities of Aleppo and Hama, spread across many faculties. The relationship was not the usual one between a supervisor and students; it was one integrated team. We shared out the roles openly, by each member's interest and specialty, alongside theoretical training on writing each part of a paper and on scientific publishing, its requirements and every one of its stages. We met every week to discuss, disagree, agree and improve. Most importantly, we learned from each other: everyone was a teacher and everyone was a learner. This spirit is what made the difference.

## Second: integrated sciences, and science as one whole

This team is the strongest proof that science is one whole that cannot be divided, even though it branches into specialties. No one can reach a complete piece of research alone, and many disciplines together are the only way to do it. In this team, every specialty added a piece of the truth, and every member completed another's role.

## Third: thanks and appreciation

Our deep thanks go to the University of Aleppo and its presidency, who believed in us and gave the support needed to complete this research. Without that support, this work would not have seen the light.

Special thanks to everyone who helped quietly and sincerely. Their names do not appear, but their fingerprints are clear in every detail. You are the real partners in this success.

And our deep thanks to every member of this team.

## Fourth: a message to every ambitious young Syrian

This post is not only about pride. It is an open invitation to every young man and woman in Syria:

- Do not wait for someone to teach you; teach yourself.
- Do not work alone; find a team.
- Do not fear many disciplines; they are your strength.
- Do not think international publishing is out of reach. We did it, and you can too. Syria's youth are capable, and this is the proof.

This research is one hundred percent Syrian, and this team is a model of what young people can do when they are given opportunity and trust.
