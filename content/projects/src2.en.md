## Why this camp

- To understand modern research methodology.
- To be guided step by step from an idea to a published paper.
- To get specialist academic supervision.
- To build working relationships with a local and international research community.

## Who it is for

Final-year undergraduate and master's students who have a research idea and want to learn the research process properly, with direct support from professors and researchers and a community that widens their horizons.

## How we hold quality

- **Before and after tests** measure what each participant has learned.
- **80% attendance** is required for the graduation certificate.
- **Regular satisfaction surveys** on content, trainers and logistics.
- **An open feedback channel** to the camp management.
- **A 24-point evaluation** of each research project before it goes to a journal.

## Run by the first camp's graduates

The second camp is organised by a volunteer team in management, monitoring and evaluation, and media, at whose core are graduates of the [first camp](/en/projects/src1/).
