## Why this camp

- To understand modern research methodology.
- To be guided step by step from an idea to a published paper.
- To get specialist academic supervision from experienced PhDs.
- To build working relationships with a local and international research community.

## Who it is for

Final-year undergraduate and master's students who have a research idea and want to learn the research process properly, with direct support from professors and professional researchers and a community that widens their horizons.

## What participants get

- Direct contact with specialist PhDs across research fields.
- Close academic supervision of the research, step by step.
- Support to raise the quality of the research and prepare it for leading international journals.
- A local research network that keeps them part of a lasting community.
- A complete research guide built from the workshop content.
- The chance to join future research teams.
