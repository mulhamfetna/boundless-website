## Why this camp

- To understand modern research methodology.
- To be guided step by step from an idea to a published paper.
- To get specialist academic supervision.
- To build working relationships with a local and international research community.

## Who it is for

Final-year undergraduate and master's students who have a research idea and want to learn the research process properly, with direct support from professors and researchers and a community that widens their horizons.
