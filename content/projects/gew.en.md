## About the week

Global Entrepreneurship Week is a yearly event that celebrates and connects entrepreneurs around the world. In Syria it is organised by Startup Syria with local partners in each city.

## Our role in Aleppo

As a local partner, we run at least three events in Aleppo during the week, following the programme's shared identity and standards.

## Events

Boundless's events in Aleppo, their dates and how to register will be announced here soon.

<p class="notice">These events are part of Global Entrepreneurship Week Syria, organised by Startup Syria within the global initiative led by the Global Entrepreneurship Network (GEN), and are run by Boundless.</p>
