## About the week

Global Entrepreneurship Week is a global initiative held every November and led by the Global Entrepreneurship Network (GEN). In Syria it is organised and coordinated by Startup Syria, with local partners in Syrian cities.

## Researchers and entrepreneurs at one table

Boundless took part as a local partner in Aleppo. We built our programme on one idea: research and startups are two sides of the same coin, and both need funders' trust and a strong team. The two workshops covered two tracks:

- **Building trust with funders** through a professional digital presence.
- **Building an effective team** by choosing partners well and organising how they work together.

The workshops were for founders of startups and community initiatives, and for researchers. Registration for all events was through Startup Syria's official platform.

## After the workshop

Participants in Personal Branding 101 received a 7-day workbook, a tracker and badges for the four levels. The files are open to everyone on the [workshop kit page](/en/pb101/).
