## About the week

Global Entrepreneurship Week is a global initiative held every November and led by the Global Entrepreneurship Network (GEN). In Syria it is organised and coordinated by Startup Syria, with local partners in Syrian cities.

## Boundless in Aleppo

Boundless takes part as a local partner in Aleppo. Our events and their dates will be announced here soon.

Registration for all events of the week is only through Startup Syria's official platform.
