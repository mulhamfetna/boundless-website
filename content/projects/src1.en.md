## The published paper

> **Design and evaluation of triple-doped nano-hydroxyapatite (Mg/Si/Zn-HAP) as multi-step intelligent scaffold for bone healing**
> Hybrid Advances · 2026 · [doi.org/10.1016/j.hybadv.2026.100657](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.hybadv.2026.100657)

The research develops nano-scale "smart scaffolds" that help bones heal faster and better, opening new directions in biomedical treatment.

## Three new papers in progress

The work didn't stop when the camp ended. Graduates of the first camp are now working on three new papers, in multidisciplinary teams of 22, 5 and 3 researchers.

## From trainees to organisers

The clearest sign of the camp's impact: several of its graduates became core members of the team that runs the second camp.
